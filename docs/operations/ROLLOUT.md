# Rollout acceptance and evidence

The current deliverable is local engineering evidence, not an authorization to host, publish or upgrade a chain program. Maintain an explicit distinction between observations already collected and future operator evidence.

## Proposed progression

All four stages below are **proposed acceptance gates, not observed service levels or completed stages**. Advancing requires the release owner's separate approval. Customer hosting, additional users or distribution also require real configuration, assigned contacts, licensing approval and current runtime/security review. Devnet remains the only Solana environment; no stage authorizes Mainnet, npm publication or deployment.

Measure latency and terminal errors over a predeclared valid workload using the same version, environment binding and archive. Exclude deliberate fault injection and expected input/authentication denials from the healthy-request denominator, but report those tests separately and require every denial/closure case to pass. A terminal 5xx, timeout or unexpected response is an error. No sample or observation window below has yet been collected for a hosted service.

| Stage | Minimum evidence window and sample | Proposed healthy-request gate | Operator, security and rollback gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Internal testing | 30 continuous minutes; at least 100 valid read-only requests, plus the complete synthetic/wallet-rejection/expiry/recovery matrix | At least 98% expected responses; p95 at most 4 seconds; liveness 100% while the owned process is running | Release owner and service operator review all failures; zero unreviewed high/critical runtime findings; prior matching archive restores and passes smoke within 15 minutes. |
| Controlled pilot | 7 consecutive days; at most 5 named pilot operators; at least 500 valid requests total across desktop/mobile scenarios | At least 99% expected responses; p95 at most 4 seconds; zero unknown network/program identity successes | Assigned service operator and security contact; real-wallet acceptance recorded without unattended signing; daily error review; rollback drill within 15 minutes. |
| Limited developer access | 14 consecutive days; at most 10 owner-approved integrations; at least 1,000 valid requests total | At least 99% expected responses; p95 at most 4 seconds; no unresolved regression in protected delivery or purchase recovery | Assigned integration-support owner; acknowledge a reported blocking integration issue within one business day; review dependency exceptions weekly and before expiry; rollback within 30 minutes. |
| Broader availability | 30 consecutive days after the limited stage; at least 5,000 valid requests total and a reviewed capacity sample within configured limits | At least 99.5% expected responses; p95 at most 4 seconds; no unreviewed capacity saturation or timeout trend | Release owner approves scope and support coverage; security contact acknowledges a suspected critical incident within 4 hours during declared coverage; rollback within 30 minutes; rehearse loss of process-local sessions. |

At every stage require zero unauthorized protected responses, zero accepted wrong-network/program identities, zero automatic wallet/sign/send actions and 100% closure on expiry, stale data, offline/account change and RPC failure in the acceptance matrix. Stop progression for a hash/binding mismatch, unreviewed high/critical runtime finding, expired review/exception, missing custody evidence for a proposed chain action, or a failed rollback. Reviewed exceptions must retain their exact scope and deadlines; these targets do not silently waive them. A four-second p95 is a proposed operating target beneath the existing five-second reader deadline, not a promise inferred from passing tests.

Record the observation interval, sample definition/count, expected-response rate, p50/p95 or bucket bounds, terminal error counts, denial-matrix results, support incidents, security decisions and rollback time in the stage receipt. If a minimum sample is not reached, extend observation rather than presenting an undersized sample as a passed gate. Change proposed thresholds only through an explicit reviewed decision before measuring the next stage.

Before any future hosted rollout, collect at least a 30-minute representative staging observation with liveness/readiness status counts, readiness latency bounds or actual event-derived p50/p95, terminal RPC success/failure and timeout counts, auth/session failure counts, denied protected requests and process restart behavior. Per-endpoint RPC attempt/failover counters are unavailable in this release; do not infer them from terminal success. Use the available saturation gauges and [diagnostics](RUNBOOKS.md#available-diagnostics), or record separately reviewed provider evidence. Proposed targets are zero wrong-network/identity successes, zero unauthorized protected responses and zero automatic wallet/sign/send actions. Record actual measurements and agreed latency/error targets; no production SLO has yet been measured or promised.

Exercise rollback to the previous matching archive/config/runtime and confirm reauthentication, preserved purchase recovery and closed protected access on RPC failure/expiry. Record time to restore, exact hashes and any manual steps. Future deployment needs an owner/custodian decision outside these local commands. Stop a rollout when evidence is missing, a review deadline expires, a hash/binding differs, or custody/configuration cannot be established.
